Re-use

Whether you plan to make a remix or use my music for other content, this page should answer your questions related to re-using my music. If it doesn't, please contact me!

Remixing

I allow remixing/covering of any of my works, so long as:

- The original title and "remix" (or any of its synonyms) appear anywhere in the title
- It isn't stated as an "official remix" (unless authorized by myself)
- My brand name (TRDZ7) or logo (top-left of the page) are visible in the title OR thumbnail/cover artwork. I should also be linked in any descriptions/credits.
- The remix/cover is sufficiently different from the original.

If you wish to make an official remix, please contact me directly :)

Re-purposing

You may use my music for monetized YouTube or other content:

- My work shouldn't be the main focus of the video (no re-uploading)
- My artist name (TRDZ7) must be seen in the description or credits
- The song name should be visible with the credit.

In case of any doubts, it's probably always best to contact me. My response time is same-day.
